Featured Case Study · Jan 2026 — May 2026

Rareduft: optimizing a full-funnel Meta Ads account.

Performance Marketing / Content Strategy — Freelance

₹46K+Total Meta Ads spend
CONTEXT

Scale the signal, not the spend.

Managed 9 campaigns, 10 ad sets and 38 ad creatives for a D2C fragrance brand while monitoring performance across the full funnel.

DIAGNOSIS

One ad set was absorbing the budget.

Identified an ad set consuming 56% of total spend (₹26K+) while returning only 0.37× ROAS.

ACTION

Reallocate based on performance.

Recommended budget redistribution and used creative-level analysis to identify stronger signals, including the top-performing UGC creative.

RESULT

4.68× ROAS on the strongest creative.

The UGC result provided data-backed insight that shaped future creative strategy and content direction.
